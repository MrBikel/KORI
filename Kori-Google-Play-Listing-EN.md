## Full Description

Kori (কড়ি) is a modern, 100% offline-first personal financial record-keeping application designed to keep track of every penny with precision and absolute privacy.

With Kori, your financial records never leave your device. There are no remote database servers, no tracking ads, and no mandatory accounts.

★ Key Features:

1. Fast & Comprehensive Expense Tracking:
- Record daily earnings and expenses across multiple accounts (Cash, Bank, Mobile Wallets).
- Itemize receipts and grocery lists directly within transactions.
- Filter and search past records effortlessly in both Bengali and English.

2. Debt & Receivable Management:
- Keep transparent ledgers of who owes you money and who you owe.
- Track partial installments and payment acknowledgments accurately.

3. Bank-Style Statements & Invoices:
- Generate itemized periodic financial statements with opening, running, and closing balances.
- Export clean, high-resolution PDF statements and invoices rendered with bundled Bengali and English typefaces.

4. Frugality & Budget Goals:
- Set spending thresholds for individual categories.
- Receive on-device, algorithmic financial insights and budget alerts without external AI dependencies.

5. Data Ownership & Offline Portability:
- No login or registration required—open the app and start recording instantly.
- Create full versioned JSON backups locally and restore them anytime.
- Import and export standard UTF-8 CSV files with duplicate row detection and transactional safety.
- Protect your records with optional on-device biometric or PIN lock.

Kori is a personal bookkeeping diary and does not provide loans, credit, banking, or regulated investment advice.
