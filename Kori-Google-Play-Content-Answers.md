- **Does the app allow users to create an account?** **No**.

- **Privacy Policy Required:** **Yes**.

### 10. Permission Declarations
- `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`: Declared for local user-scheduled budgeting reminders.
- `USE_BIOMETRIC`: Declared for on-device app lock via Android BiometricPrompt.
- Dangerous storage/contacts/SMS permissions: **None requested**.

- **Track:** Closed Testing (Internal Testing / Closed Alpha) with at least 20 testers for 14 days to meet Google Play personal developer requirements, followed by Open Testing or Production release.
