---

### 2. Spacing and Dimensions
- **Screen Horizontal Padding**: `20dp`
- **Section Gap**: `28dp`
- **Surface Padding**: `18dp`
- **Row Vertical Padding**: `14dp`
- **Component Gap**: `12dp`
- **Corner Radius**: `16dp` (Cards), `20dp` (Summary Surfaces)
- **Primary Button Height**: `52dp`
- **Minimum Touch Target**: `48dp` (meets M3 accessibility standards)

---

### 3. Visual Restrictions Enforced
- Zero pure black `#000000`
- Zero leather, gold, skeuomorphic, or heavy gradient textures
- No nested cards or low-contrast text
- Maximum 1 dominant summary surface per viewport
- Maximum 2 primary action buttons
